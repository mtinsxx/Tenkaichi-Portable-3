# Contributions, testing and respectful interaction

BT3-PSP is an **unofficial experimental PSP port**, maintained in this repository by [mtinsxx](https://github.com/mtinsxx), based on the earlier projects credited in [README.md](README.md) and [THIRD_PARTY.md](THIRD_PARTY.md). It is not an official release from the game's rights holders or from the upstream port developers.

- **Development is currently maintainer-only.** Do not open public bug reports, suggestions, issues, discussions or unsolicited pull requests at this stage. Public support and contributions are paused until the maintainer explicitly reopens them.
- The maintainer may decline support for third-party builds and tests. This is an experimental project, not a stable game release.
- If you contact the maintainer, focus on technical facts and be respectful. Insults, threats, harassment, impersonation and repeated unwanted contact are not welcome.
- Do not upload copyrighted game assets, ISO files, proprietary game executables, private keys or credentials.
- Preserve the original authors' attribution and applicable license notices. Maintaining this PSP repository is not a claim of owning the original game or its decompilation.
- Development builds are experimental and may fail to compile, render correctly or reach playable frame rates.
- If the repository is public, these preferences do **not** prohibit anyone from independently downloading, compiling or testing the public source where they have the legal rights to do so.

Security reports involving secrets must never publish the secret value in issues, PRs or logs. Use private vulnerability reporting if enabled, or wait for a private channel approved by the maintainer.

See [publication/privacy checklist](docs/psp/PUBLICATION_CHECKLIST_PTBR.md) before any change of repository visibility.
